# Marketing Audience & SEO Brief

Source of truth for every marketing page (homepage, /vs comparison pages, blog, about).
Synthesized 2026-07-23 from four parallel deep-research passes (warm follower, cold searcher,
cultural/market, SEO). Claims are cited in the research transcripts; the strongest are peer-reviewed,
and the shaky ones are flagged **[weak]** so we never build copy on sand.

## The core insight that ties it all together

> **You were never the problem, and you were never meant to do this alone.**

The research (peer-reviewed) says she is a serial re-starter, not a beginner: ~1 in 2 quit within
six months, and weight-cycling leaves **shame and internalized stigma**, treating weight as "a
measure of character." Every failed attempt escalates. So "marathon not a race" is not a slogan, it
is the literal antidote to the shame cycle. Name the cycle out loud instead of selling past it.

## Warm-follower profile (home + about)

- **She holds TWO body ideals at once** (mainstream thin AND Latina curvy) = real internal conflict.
  DO NOT lead with "Latinas love curves, so body positivity." That's the naive read the research
  contradicts. What converts: **representation + body functionality + naming the shame**.
- **Marianismo is the objection engine.** Self-care reads as *taking from her family*. Don't fight
  it, use it: "a fuller cup, not less love." Self-care as service, not subtraction.
- **"Nosotras" is the delivery model, not branding.** Stephanie is structurally a *promotora at
  scale* (a trusted woman from the community, not an authority above it) — a model that roughly
  doubled healthy-behavior odds in real Latina programs. Use first-person-plural feminine: nosotras,
  juntas, vamos.
- **Faith = gratitude, never doctrine.** "Blessed" reads as thankfulness for a body that carries you
  and a community that holds you. Never a sermon, never a denominational gate.
- **Conversion cues, in proven order:** (1) real member before/afters of varied realistic Latina
  bodies, first names + their words; (2) Stephanie visibly present ("I answer," not "we have a
  team"); (3) member count as belonging ("256 women, and a seat for you"); (4) representation stated
  plainly; (5) beginners shown next to transformations so the newcomer sees an on-ramp.

### Warm objection -> counter
| She says | Counter |
|---|---|
| "I can't justify another subscription." | Per-day + ownership: "less than a coffee, and unlike the gym you quit, this comes with Stephanie and women who notice if you disappear." Low-friction trial. |
| "I've started a hundred times." | Meet the shame: "you're not the exception, you're the 1 in 2. What broke wasn't you, it was doing it alone." |
| "I don't have time, my family needs me." | Use the family frame: "20 minutes. You take better care of everyone when you're not running on empty." |
| "Not made for a body like mine." | Representation + functionality: real member bodies, "a body like yours belongs here." |
| "Just another influencer cashing in?" | Keep it relational not transactional. Her origin story ("I built this because screenshotting food labels into ChatGPT is not a life") is the antidote. |

## Cold-searcher profile (/vs + blog)

**Comparison criteria in priority order:** (1) logging friction/speed, (2) **does it have MY food**,
(3) *consistent* accuracy (stable, not lab-perfect), (4) price / free-tier completeness, (5) coaching/
accountability, (6) **language support** (regional food vocab, not just translated UI).
**Lead /vs pages with #2 and #6 — every competitor is weakest there.**

**The photo-scan lesson (critical): DO NOT over-claim accuracy.** Cal AI's uncited "90%" got it
ridiculed on Reddit and it was pulled from the App Store (April 2026) for deceptive billing. Photo
apps run 30-50% error on mixed/saucy/regional dishes — which *is* Latin home cooking. Position as
**"accurate on the foods you actually eat,"** shown with a real dish + cooked/raw + verified DB.
Brand rule holds everywhere: frame the scan as **"her method, automated," never "AI."**

### Cold objection -> counter
| Objection | Counter |
|---|---|
| "Why pay vs free MyFitnessPal?" | MFP free is now a 5-food/day demo with barcode paywalled. "Free MFP already isn't free for real use, and it doesn't have your food or a coach." |
| "Is the photo scan accurate?" | Honesty as differentiator: accurate on YOUR dishes, cooked/raw, verified DB. Never "90% on everything." |
| "Will it work for MY food?" (decisive) | Show real logs of pozole/tamales/pupusas/bandeja paisa by component. A live demo beats any claim. |
| "I'll get trapped in a sub I can't cancel." | Visible one-tap self-serve cancel + transparent pricing as a stated promise. A moat, not a footnote. |
| "An app can't replace a coach." | Don't sell app-vs-coach: "her actual method, in her voice, at app price." |

## Voice & language guide (both segments)

- **Default to tú. Never usted** (usted reads corporate/cold for a peer creator brand).
- **Nosotras / juntas / vamos** over "you should." First-person-plural feminine.
- **Neutral (Mexican-leaning) Spanish for UI/system copy; Stephanie's real Spanglish for creator
  surfaces** (coach voice, videos, community). **Never machine-translate** — this audience punishes
  translated-sounding Spanish. Native bilingual review required.
- **Dialect flag:** heavy Spanglish reads native to US Latinas but "foreign/pocho" in LATAM. Keep it
  to US-facing surfaces. `vos` (Argentina/Uruguay) exists but tú is the safe single choice.
- **Body positivity = function + heritage + representation.** Not appearance, not before/after shame,
  not "bounce back." Thread health and self-acceptance together (the tension she already lives).
- **Cultural food = an asset to work WITH, not eliminate.** "We make your mom's food fit your
  macros," not chicken-and-broccoli.

## US vs LATAM divergence (one audience emotionally, two on money/infrastructure)

- **Payment (biggest split):** US cards work today. LATAM is A2A/cash-voucher dominant (~30%
  unbanked; credit cards only ~42% of volume). Cards-only silently excludes most of LATAM. Local
  rails (Pix, OXXO/SPEI, Mercado Pago) are correctly deferred to Phase 3 — this confirms it's a real
  gate for the LATAM half. **[corrected] the "72% without credit cards" figure could not be verified;
  stop quoting it. The verified facts still support the conclusion.**
- **Price:** LATAM willingness-to-pay runs 20-50% lower. A flat $19.97 USD is likely too high there;
  PPP/local pricing needed for LATAM conversion.
- **Competitors:** LATAM = **Fitia dominates** nutrition (and is entering the US). US = MyFitnessPal/
  Noom/Lose It, all with the cultural-food gap.
- **Channel:** WhatsApp is a first-class channel (esp. LATAM), not email. Build mobile-first,
  low-bandwidth.

## Health stakes (mission, cite-safe)
US Hispanic adults: ~13% more likely to have diabetes, obesity >=35% in 34 states, only 33.4% meet
activity guidelines (vs 47.6% non-Hispanic white). Use as **health-and-function-first mission**, not
fear/shame copy (fear conflicts with the body-positive brand).

## /vs build order (reordered by CONSUMER intent, both SEO agents agree)

"Lenus/Trainerize alternative" searches are **coach B2B intent, the wrong buyer.** Reorder:

1. **/vs/myfitnesspal** (build first) — HIGH evidenced demand (MFP redesign + paywalling spike).
   Angle: MFP's DB is user-generated and wrong for Latin food; its Spanish is a translated UI over an
   Anglo DB. Attack the *data behind the Spanish*, not "it doesn't speak Spanish" (it does). Win the
   modified term: "MyFitnessPal alternative for Latin food / en español / for Latinas."
2. **/vs/cal-ai** — HIGH demand. Angle: photo apps collapse on Latin home cooking (30-50% error);
   ours reads her food + cooked/raw. Her method, automated (never "AI").
3. **/vs/fitia** — highest strategic fit, moderate demand **[partly weak]**. Angle: Fitia is a
   tracker with no soul — no creator, no coach, no workouts, no community, and it isn't *hers*. The
   defensive page that protects against the incumbent.
4. **/vs/lenus** — LOW cold-SEO demand (coach B2B SERP) but HIGH warm/branded value. Angle: "the app
   Stephanie built so she'd never depend on Lenus again." Traffic is social/email, not organic.
5. **DROP** /vs/trainerize, /vs/truecoach, /vs/everfit (coach intent, wrong audience).

## SEO winnability
- English comparison head terms = red ocean (DR 50-80 farms). Win only the modified long tail
  (`+ "for Latinas / en espanol / for Latin food"`).
- **Spanish nutrition SERPs = winnable**: one strong domain (Fitia), one faceless upstart (Nutrola),
  a pile of junk apps. Our unfair advantage is **E-E-A-T**: Stephanie is a real PN-certified
  nutritionist with documented results — put authorship + credentials on every post (YMYL demands it).
- **Own branded terms NOW** ("Stephanie Pantoja," "Thick & Fit") — greenfield, she isn't indexed for
  the app yet.
- Use hreflang EN/ES + Recipe/HowTo/FAQ schema the junk apps don't implement.
- Monitor **Nutrola** (running the exact Latin-food play, but faceless/no coach = the whitespace we own).

## Honest flags carried forward
- Search volumes are **inferred from SERP/content density, not measured** — validate the [I] topics
  with Ahrefs/SEMrush (ES-MX + ES-US) before committing production hours.
- "Lenus 30% cut" is a **trainer-side, competitor-sourced** claim — business case only, not consumer copy.
- Cal AI possibly MyFitnessPal-owned now (one competitor source) — verify before using in copy.
- Never cite Fitia's or Nutrola's self-claims as fact (they self-rank #1).
- Cite-safe neutral sources: TechCrunch, Biolayne, Journal of Human Nutrition & Dietetics (Oct 2025),
  Frontiers in Nutrition, Healthline, Fortune, AARP en espanol, Adapty, Apple Community, MFP forum.
