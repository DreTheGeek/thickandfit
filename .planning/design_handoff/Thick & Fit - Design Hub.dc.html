01 The Brief
What we're building

A bilingual (EN/ES) coaching app that replaces Stephanie's manual Lenus delivery - structured workouts with her filmed demos, the lowest-friction nutrition tracking in the category, a living community, and an AI coach in her voice. She owns the brand, data, relationship, and roadmap.

The wedge is accurate nutrition. The moat is honest billing. The retention engine is community. Her brand is the acquisition engine.

The design rule

Keep Lenus's IA so migrated clients find Home, Clients, and the program builder right where they expect. Replace the soulless 2019 SaaS execution entirely. The beige is gone.

Banned on sight

Centered gradient heroes · 3-column icon grids · floating device mockups · gradient blobs · Inter-only type · purple-on-white · "unlock your potential / seamless / empower / game-changing."

05 Build Phases · Design Coverage
PHASE 1
PRD 00–12 · main
Foundation, bilingual infra, marketing shell + waitlist, auth + 5-role RBAC + onboarding, Stripe honest billing, pricing tiers + firewall, subscriber dashboard, the full workout system (library, builder, player, logging, substitution, overload), Lenus migration of 256 clients.
PHASE 2
PRD 13–30 · phase-2
The nutrition wedge: macro calculator, US + LATAM food DB, barcode, AI photo-to-macro, cooked/uncooked, meal plans, recipe engine. Community feed + groups + DMs + broadcasts + challenges. Progress photos, measurements, check-ins, habits. Multi-form builder, mid-ticket workflow.
PHASE 3
PRD 31–47 · phase-3
AI Coach (text → voice clone), full coach toolbox, GHL drip, digital store, $2.99 follow-along add-on, affiliate, gamification, push + email, coach analytics, branding settings, SEO/AEO, Capacitor + Apple Health, follow-along video, LATAM local payments, MCP API.
