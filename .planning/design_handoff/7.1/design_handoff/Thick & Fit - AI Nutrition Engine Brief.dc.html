00

The one rule that makes or breaks accuracy

Non-negotiable

The LLM identifies the food and estimates the portion. The LLM does NOT invent the macro numbers. Every calorie/protein/carb/fat value shown to a client must come from a real nutrition database, keyed off what the model recognized.

This is exactly how Cal AI and MyFitnessPal work. Cal AI uses a GPT-4-Vision-class model to recognize the dish + estimate portion, then maps it to a nutrition database for the actual grams. MyFitnessPal's moat was never a model — it's a ~20M-item food database; the AI sits on top. If you let the LLM free-hand the numbers, you get confident, wrong data — the fastest way to lose Steph's trust. Accuracy is the wedge; the database is the product.

    shake"                                                                               ├→ log entry

Why this wins: the model only does what models are good at (recognition + language). Numbers are grounded in validated data, cached, and correctable by the user ("Fix results"). The Thick & Fit coaching layer is yours alone — no competitor has Steph's goal-aware logic on top.

03

Real API reference (endpoints, auth, limits)

USDA FoodData Central

FREE · PUBLIC DOMAIN

Government-validated, commercial-use OK, no licensing fees. ~400K foods across Foundation, SR Legacy, Branded, and Survey (FNDDS). US-focused, English only, updates quarterly. Best free base for generic/whole foods. Get a free key at fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-key-signup. Limit: 1,000 req/hr/IP (DEMO_KEY only 30/hr — don't ship it). Returns HTTP 429 when exceeded.

# search
GET https://api.nal.usda.gov/fdc/v1/foods/search?query=grilled+chicken+breast&dataType=Foundation,SR%20Legacy&pageSize=5&api_key=$KEY
# full nutrient detail for one food
GET https://api.nal.usda.gov/fdc/v1/food/171077?api_key=$KEY
# nutrient numbers you care about: 208=kcal 203=protein 204=fat 205=carbs 291=fiber 269=sugar 307=sodium

⚠ Gaps: no image recognition, no barcode, no natural-language, thin on restaurant/branded chains. Default missing nutrients to null, not 0.

Nutritionix (Track API v2)

PAID · TRIAL VIA SALES

1.9M+ foods, the strongest branded + chain-restaurant catalog, and a purpose-built natural-language endpoint. 24 languages, imperial units, integrates with Apple Health. Auth via x-app-id + x-app-key headers. Note: the old public free tier was discontinued (abuse); you now request a trial through their sales team, and enterprise plans start around $1,850/mo. Worth it once you need restaurant accuracy.

# natural language → macros (the "10 chicken nuggets and a shake" endpoint)
POST https://trackapi.nutritionix.com/v2/natural/nutrients
     headers: x-app-id, x-app-key, Content-Type: application/json
     body:    { "query": "10 chicken nuggets and a protein shake" }
# instant autocomplete (search box) · UPC/barcode lookup
GET  https://trackapi.nutritionix.com/v2/search/instant?query=oatmeal
GET  https://trackapi.nutritionix.com/v2/search/item?upc=049000042566

✔ Use for: the describe-it feature (turnkey), restaurant meals, and US branded/UPC when Open Food Facts misses.

Open Food Facts

FREE · NO KEY · ODbL

Crowd-sourced, 2.5M+ packaged products, strongest in Europe. No API key, no signup — just send a descriptive User-Agent identifying the app (e.g. ThickAndFit/1.0 (dev@teamthickandfit.com)). Returns a nutriments object (per 100g and per serving), Nutri-Score, NOVA processing group, allergens, and product images. Because it's crowd-sourced, check each field exists before trusting it.

# barcode lookup — request only the fields you need
GET https://world.openfoodfacts.org/api/v2/product/3017624010701.json?fields=product_name,brands,nutriments,serving_size,image_url,nutriscore_grade
     header: User-Agent: ThickAndFit/1.0 (contact@teamthickandfit.com)
# status:1 = found, status:0 = not in DB → fall back to Nutritionix UPC or manual add

⚠ Rate limits: 15 req/min/IP for product reads, 10 req/min/IP for search — never wire it to search-as-you-type; you'll be blocked. Cache aggressively (see §07). For heavy volume, self-host or use their daily data export.

Also worth knowing: Edamam (NLP + 900K foods + 680K UPCs + 2.3M recipes; free → $999/mo) is a solid single-vendor alternative. API Ninjas Nutrition (absorbed CalorieNinjas) is a cheap lightweight NL endpoint for MVP. Chomp is barcode-focused with a free tier. Market context to keep in mind: US nutrition apps were a $2.14B market in 2024, and ~70% of users abandon a tracker within two weeks if logging feels slow — which is exactly why the snap/describe flow, not manual entry, is the whole game.

04

Data model (Supabase / Postgres)

Minimum tables to back the flow. Everything the app shows is derived from food_logs joined to foods, reconciled against the user's nutrition_targets. Cache external lookups in foods so you never pay for or wait on the same food twice.

foods                         -- canonical + cached external foods (your growing DB)
  id uuid pk · name text · brand text · source enum('usda','nutritionix','off','custom','ai')
  source_ref text            -- fdcId / nix id / barcode
  serving_qty numeric · serving_unit text · grams_per_serving numeric
  kcal numeric · protein_g numeric · carbs_g numeric · fat_g numeric
  fiber_g numeric · sugar_g numeric · sodium_mg numeric
  verified bool · created_by uuid · created_at timestamptz

food_logs                     -- one row per logged item
  id uuid pk · user_id uuid fk · food_id uuid fk · logged_at timestamptz
  meal enum('breakfast','lunch','dinner','snack')
  servings numeric           -- multiplier on foods.serving_*
  source enum('photo','describe','barcode','search','manual')
  photo_path text            -- Supabase Storage key, nullable
  ai_confidence numeric · user_edited bool

ai_estimates                  -- audit trail: what the model guessed vs what was logged
  id uuid pk · food_log_id uuid fk · model text · input_type text
  raw_response jsonb · matched_food_id uuid · portion_reasoning text

nutrition_targets             -- per user, set at onboarding, recomputed on goal change
  user_id uuid pk · kcal int · protein_g int · carbs_g int · fat_g int
  goal enum('fat_loss','recomp','gain','maintain') · goal_weight_lb numeric
  deadline date · activity_level text · pcos bool · diet text

Targets math: compute BMR with Mifflin–St Jeor, multiply by activity factor for TDEE, then apply the goal delta (e.g. −500 kcal/day for fat loss) and project the goal-weight date from the deficit. Recompute whenever weight, goal, or deadline changes. This is what powers the "on pace for 195 lb by October" line in the UI.

05

Model contracts (exact prompts + JSON)

Standardize on strict JSON output from every model call so the backend can parse deterministically. Three calls: (A) vision, (B) natural-language, (C) the coach layer. Numbers from A/B are treated as guesses to be reconciled against the DB — see §07.

A · Vision (photo → items + portions). Send the image + this system prompt to Claude Sonnet / GPT-4o:

SYSTEM: You are a food-recognition engine for a fitness app. Look at the meal photo.
Return ONLY minified JSON, no prose:
{"dish":string,"confidence":0-1,"items":[
   {"name":string,"portion_desc":string,"est_grams":number,"basis":string}]}
Identify each distinct food. Estimate portion in grams from visual cues
(plate size, utensils, references). "basis" = how you judged the portion.
Do NOT output calories or macros — those are looked up downstream.

B · Natural language ("what I ate" → items). Text-only; hand each parsed item to the DB. If you use Nutritionix, its /natural/nutrients does B and the lookup in one call — prefer that at scale.

SYSTEM: Parse the user's meal description into structured items.
Return ONLY minified JSON:
{"items":[{"name":string,"qty":number,"unit":string,"prep":string}]}
Normalize units. Resolve counts ("a dozen"→12). Keep brand names if given.
Do NOT estimate macros.
USER: "10 chicken nuggets, a 10oz ribeye and 5 fried eggs"

C · Coach layer (grounded totals → Steph's guidance). Runs after the DB gives real numbers. This is the Thick & Fit differentiator.

SYSTEM: You are Coach Steph. Given the logged meal's real macros, the user's
remaining daily targets, their goal, and flags (pcos, budget, diet), write ONE short,
warm, specific line and an optional swap. Return JSON:
{"health_score":1-10,"fits_plan":bool,"line":string,"swap":string|null}
Rules: if pcos, prefer lower-starch/higher-fiber swaps. If budget, keep swaps cheap.
Never shame. Reference the goal ("keeps you on pace for 195 lb").

07

Grounding & accuracy strategy

  • Reconcile, don't trust. For each recognized item: search your foods table → then USDA/Nutritionix. Take the DB macros per 100g, scale by the model's est_grams. The model supplies what and how much; the DB supplies the per-gram truth.
  • Cache every external hit into foods (with source + source_ref). Second time anyone logs that food, it's instant and free — this is how you respect Open Food Facts' 15 req/min limit and cut LLM/Nutritionix cost.
  • Confidence + the "Fix results" loop. Show ai_confidence; when low, prompt the user to confirm portion. Every correction updates the log and can seed a verified custom food. This is Cal AI's "Fix Results" — and your correction data compounds into a better DB over time.
  • Portion is where accuracy lives. Photos over-/under-estimate grams; let users tap a serving stepper (the numpad screen) to adjust. Default to conservative portions.
  • Custom foods & Steph's recipes. Let clients (and Steph) save foods/meals once and re-log instantly — fills the gaps USDA/OFF miss, and makes her meal-plan recipes one-tap loggable.
08

The Thick & Fit layer (what no competitor has)

Cal AI stops at "here are your macros." Thick & Fit continues: goal-aware coaching computed from nutrition_targets and Steph's rules.

  • Fits-your-plan: after logging, show remaining kcal/macros and whether they're still on pace for their goal-weight deadline (Mifflin–St Jeor projection).
  • Steph swaps: the coach model suggests a realistic substitution ("cauliflower rice saves ~180 cal, keeps protein"). Tune to tier and to flags.
  • PCOS & budget aware: PCOS clients get lower-starch/higher-fiber/higher-fat guidance; budget clients get cheap swaps and simple 3–5 ingredient meals.
  • Tier-driven: habits, coach-call reminders (Calendly), and depth of guidance scale with high/mid/low ticket.
  • Bilingual & units: every string EN/ES; imperial + metric. Store canonical values in metric grams; convert at display.
