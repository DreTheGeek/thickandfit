# Your walkthrough

What we cover on the call, and what to keep afterwards. Roughly an hour, recorded so you can rewatch
any part of it.

**The goal is not for you to learn the whole app.** It is for you to be able to run a normal day
without calling anyone. There are about twenty screens. You need six.

---

## The six screens you actually use

Everything else can wait, possibly forever.

| screen | what it is for | how often |
|---|---|---|
| **Clients** | everyone you coach, and their whole history | every day |
| **Waiting on a program** | who has signed up and is waiting on you | every day |
| **Inbox** | messages from your members | every day |
| **Programs** | build a workout plan and assign it | a few times a week |
| **Meal plans** | build and send a meal plan | a few times a week |
| **Intake to review** | anyone who wrote something worth a human read | when it has a number on it |

The rest of the menu is reporting, settings and admin. You are not expected to touch it.

---

## The call, in order

### 1. Logging in, and where you land (5 min)

You go to the site, sign in with your email, and you land on your coach home. If you ever end up
somewhere confusing, the Thick & Fit logo top left always takes you back.

**Save the site to your phone home screen.** It then opens like an app, full screen, no browser bar.
We will do this together on the call so it is done.

### 2. Your Clients list (15 min)

This is the screen you will live in.

- **Everyone is here**, all 265, with their history from your old platform.
- **The filters down the left** narrow it: who is active, who is at risk, who is on which plan.
- **Payment type** now tells you monthly from paid in full, which you asked for. It came from your real
  charge history, 1,539 charges going back to March 2024.
- **Click any client** to open her: her weight over time, her photos, her intake answers, what you have
  written to her, and her whole message history.

**What we practise on the call:** find one specific client by name, open her, read her history back.

### 3. Who is waiting on you (5 min)

A short list of women who finished signing up and do not have a workout plan yet, **oldest first**.

The number next to each one is how long she has been waiting. Anything over three days turns red.

**Why this screen exists:** the app tells every new member "Steph writes your plan by hand, she will
message you when it is ready." This is the list that keeps that promise honest. If it is empty, nobody
is waiting on you.

### 4. Writing a program (15 min)

The part that matters most, so we go slowly.

- Pick the exercises, set the sets and reps, save it as a plan.
- Assign the plan to a client, or to several.
- She sees it on her Train tab, with your demo video on each exercise once you have filmed them.

**The exercise library is now 250 movements, not 899.** We cut the junk and added the 26 glute
movements it never had. Search by name or filter by muscle group and equipment.

**What we practise:** build one short program together, start to finish, and assign it to a test
account so you can see exactly what she sees.

### 5. Meal plans (10 min)

Same shape: build it, assign it, she sees it under Nutrition. Your 158 recipes came across from your
old platform and are searchable.

The app already gives every member her calories and macros the moment she finishes signing up, so a
meal plan is the refinement, not the starting point. She is not sitting there with nothing while she
waits.

### 6. Messages (5 min)

Your members message you here and you reply here. Every new member already has a thread open with a
first message from you, so she never lands on an empty screen.

### 7. Questions (5 min)

Whatever you hit while we are doing the above. This is usually the most useful part.

## After the call

**You will have:** the recording, this page, and a test client account so you can look at the app the
way your members see it without touching anyone's real data.

**What we do next, only when you say so:** invite your clients in batches. Not all 264 at once, so the
first day is a handful of people rather than a wave.

---

## Things worth knowing before you start

**Your clients keep everything.** When a client accepts her invitation and creates her account, her
weight history, her photos and your old conversations are already there waiting. She does not start
from zero and neither do you.

**Nothing you do is destructive.** There is no button that deletes a client or wipes a history. If you
assign the wrong plan, reassign it. If you archive an exercise, it comes back.

**You cannot break it by clicking around.** The worst thing that happens is you end up on a screen you
did not mean to open. Go back with the logo.

**If something looks wrong, screenshot it and send it.** There is a Support button inside the app that
attaches the screenshot for you and sends it straight to us. That is faster than describing it.

## The honest list of what is not ready yet

So nothing surprises you on the call.

- **Your demo videos are not filmed.** 76 movements are on your shot list and 0 are shot. Members see
  the exercise name and written instructions, no video.
- **There is no iPhone app.** It works in a phone browser and can be saved to the home screen, which is
  what your clients will use. The App Store version is a later project and it is waiting on the D-U-N-S
  number.
- **Payments are not switched on yet.** Nothing charges anyone today.
- **43 of your clients have no payment history in the export**, so their payment type is blank. We need
  to know from you whether they were comped or whether the export was incomplete.
