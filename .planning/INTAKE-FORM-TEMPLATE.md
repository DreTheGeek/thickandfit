# Client intake form, draft

A **starting point to edit**, not a finished form. Cut anything you would not ask, add anything missing.

Your app already asks her numbers, her goals, her injury areas and the safety screen at signup, so this form does not ask those again except in the few places marked "already asked", which are there only so the form still works if you send it outside the app, and you can delete them otherwise. What is left is the part only a coach needs: her real reason for starting, what she has already tried and quit, what she actually eats in a day and will not give up, how her week is really shaped, and the safety detail a checkbox cannot capture. It is 42 questions on paper but most women answer about 30 in 5 to 8 minutes, because the cycle and postpartum and weight loss medication section disappears for anyone it does not apply to, and almost everything else is one tap.

**How to read this:** each question has the English and the Spanish, what kind of answer it takes, and
why it is there. Questions marked *already in the app* are asked at signup already; keep them only if
you send this form to someone who is not in the app yet, otherwise delete them.

---

## Why now / Por qué ahora

*The app has her numbers. It does not have her reason, and her reason is what you read back to her in week six when the scale stalls.*

**1. What made right now the moment you decided to start?**  (required)

> ES: ¿Qué hizo que este fuera el momento de empezar?

- answer type: paragraph
- Her real why, in her own words. Nothing in the app captures this. Save it and quote it back to her on a hard week.

**2. Is there a date or an event you are working toward?**

> ES: ¿Hay una fecha o un evento para el que te estás preparando?

- answer type: short answer
- A wedding, a trip, a birthday, a beach week. Tells you how fast to move and when to hold her back.

**3. Right now, how sure are you that you can stick with this for the next 4 weeks?**  (required)

> ES: Ahora mismo, ¿qué tan segura te sientes de que vas a seguir con esto las próximas 4 semanas?

- answer type: 1 to 10
- 1 to 10. Low confidence in week one is the best early warning of who quits in month one. Anyone under 6 gets a smaller starting plan, not a bigger one.

## Safety first / Primero la seguridad

*This is the section that protects her body and protects you if anything ever goes wrong. Keep all of it, and keep every answer on file.*

**4. Do any of these apply to you?**  (required)  · *already in the app*

> ES: ¿Alguna de estas te aplica?

- answer type: pick any
- Options EN: a heart condition / chest pain when you are active / dizziness or fainting / a bone or joint problem exercise could make worse / blood pressure or heart medication / none of these. ES: una condición del corazón / dolor en el pecho al hacer actividad / mareos o desmayos / un problema de huesos o articulaciones que el ejercicio pueda empeorar / medicamento para la presión o el corazón / ninguna de estas. This is the standard PAR-Q safety screen and the app already asks it at signup. Anything checked except 'none of these' means she gets her doctor's OK in writing before you program for her.

**5. Do you have any of these health conditions?**  · *already in the app*

> ES: ¿Tienes alguna de estas condiciones de salud?

- answer type: pick any
- Options EN: PCOS / insulin resistance / type 2 diabetes / thyroid condition / perimenopause or menopause / endometriosis / high blood pressure / IBS or gut issues / none of these. ES: SOP (síndrome de ovario poliquístico) / resistencia a la insulina / diabetes tipo 2 / condición de la tiroides / perimenopausia o menopausia / endometriosis / presión alta / colon irritable o problemas digestivos / ninguna de estas. Already asked in the app. You are noting it, not treating it.

**6. Do you have pain or an old injury in any of these areas?**  · *already in the app*

> ES: ¿Tienes dolor o una lesión antigua en alguna de estas zonas?

- answer type: pick any
- Options EN: knees / lower back / shoulders / wrists / hips / neck / none of these. ES: rodillas / espalda baja / hombros / muñecas / caderas / cuello / ninguna de estas. Already asked in the app, and it only tells you where. The next question tells you what to actually change.

**7. If you marked an injury, tell me in your own words: what movements hurt, when it started, and has anyone treated it?**

> ES: Si marcaste una lesión, cuéntame con tus palabras: qué movimientos te duelen, cuándo empezó y si alguien te ha atendido.

- answer type: paragraph
- This is the one that changes the program. 'Has anyone treated it' is your cue: if nobody has, she goes to a doctor or a physical therapist before you build around it. You coach movement, you do not diagnose or rehab.

**8. List every medication and supplement you take, including birth control, thyroid medication and weight loss shots.**  (required)

> ES: Escribe todos los medicamentos y suplementos que tomas, incluyendo anticonceptivos, medicina para la tiroides e inyecciones para bajar de peso.

- answer type: paragraph
- The app only offers five checkboxes, so free text catches everything else. You are never adjusting anyone's medication. You are noting what changes appetite, water retention, heart rate and energy so you read her results correctly.

**9. Has a doctor ever told you to limit or avoid exercise?**  (required)

> ES: ¿Algún doctor te ha dicho que limites o evites el ejercicio?

- answer type: yes / no
- A yes stops everything until she brings you her provider's clearance. Write down the date she gives it to you and keep it with her file.

## Cycle, pregnancy, postpartum and weight loss medication / Ciclo, embarazo, posparto y medicamentos para bajar de peso

*Most women skip this in ten seconds. For the ones it applies to, it is the difference between a plan that works and one that hurts them.*

**10. Which of these apply to you right now?**  · *already in the app*

> ES: ¿Cuál de estas te aplica ahora mismo?

- answer type: pick any
- Options EN: none of these / pregnant / postpartum, in the last 12 months / breastfeeding / trying to get pregnant / perimenopause or menopause / I would rather not say. ES: ninguna de estas / embarazada / posparto, en los últimos 12 meses / dando pecho / buscando embarazo / perimenopausia o menopausia / prefiero no decir. Set the rest of this section to only appear if she picks pregnant, postpartum or breastfeeding, or if she listed a weight loss shot above. Pregnant means you only coach what her provider has already cleared. Breastfeeding means her calories are not the same as anyone else's.

**11. Is your period regular? Does it change what you can train?**

> ES: ¿Tu periodo es regular? ¿Cambia lo que puedes entrenar?

- answer type: pick one
- Options EN: regular and it barely affects me / regular but some days are rough / irregular / I do not have a period right now / I would rather not say. ES: regular y casi no me afecta / regular pero hay días duros / irregular / ahora mismo no tengo periodo / prefiero no decir. Irregular or missing cycles are common with PCOS and with under eating. That is a reason to send her to a doctor, never a reason to guess. 'Some days are rough' tells you which week to plan lighter.

**12. How long has it been since you gave birth?**

> ES: ¿Cuánto tiempo ha pasado desde que diste a luz?

- answer type: pick one
- Options EN: less than 6 weeks / 6 weeks to 3 months / 3 to 12 months / more than a year. ES: menos de 6 semanas / de 6 semanas a 3 meses / de 3 a 12 meses / más de un año. Under 6 weeks means no program at all yet, only walking and breathing work if her provider has said so.

**13. Has your doctor cleared you to exercise?**

> ES: ¿Tu doctor ya te dio permiso para hacer ejercicio?

- answer type: yes / no
- No clearance, no program. This is the postpartum version of the safety question above and it is the one that matters legally.

**14. Since giving birth, have you noticed any of these?**

> ES: Desde que diste a luz, ¿has notado alguna de estas cosas?

- answer type: pick any
- Options EN: a bulge or a ridge down the middle of my belly when I sit up / leaking when I jump, cough or sneeze / heaviness or pressure down low / back or hip pain / none of these. ES: un bulto o una línea que salta en medio del abdomen cuando me siento / se me sale un poco de orina al saltar, toser o estornudar / sensación de peso o presión abajo / dolor de espalda o cadera / ninguna de estas. Any of the first three goes to a pelvic floor physical therapist, and you say so warmly and directly. In the meantime, no crunches, no sit ups, no full planks.

**15. If you take a weight loss shot, which one, what dose, and when did you start?**

> ES: Si te aplicas alguna inyección para bajar de peso, ¿cuál es, qué dosis y cuándo empezaste?

- answer type: short answer
- Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound, Saxenda, or a compounded version. You need the dose and the start date because a flat week or a miserable week almost always lines up with a dose increase, and without the date you will read it as her failing.

**16. If you are on one, what are you noticing?**

> ES: Si la estás usando, ¿qué has notado?

- answer type: pick any
- Options EN: barely any appetite / nausea / low energy / I cannot eat enough protein / I feel weaker in the gym / none of these. ES: casi no tengo hambre / náuseas / poca energía / no logro comer suficiente proteína / me siento más débil entrenando / ninguna de estas. Muscle loss is the real risk on these medications. Protein trouble or losing strength means you change the plan and you tell her to talk to whoever prescribes it.

## How you train / Cómo entrenas

*A plan that does not fit her actual week is the number one reason people cancel in month two.*

**17. How much experience do you have training with weights?**  (required)  · *already in the app*

> ES: ¿Cuánta experiencia tienes entrenando con pesas?

- answer type: pick one
- Options EN: brand new / some experience / experienced. ES: soy nueva en esto / tengo algo de experiencia / tengo experiencia. Already asked in the app.

**18. Honestly, how many days a week can you train?**  (required)

> ES: Con honestidad, ¿cuántos días a la semana puedes entrenar?

- answer type: pick one
- Options EN: 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 or more. ES: 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 o más. Ask for the honest number, not the ambitious one, and then program one day under what she says. A woman who hits 3 out of 3 stays. A woman who misses 2 out of 5 quits.

**19. How long can a workout realistically be?**  (required)

> ES: ¿Cuánto tiempo puede durar un entrenamiento, de verdad?

- answer type: pick one
- Options EN: 20 to 30 minutes / 30 to 45 / 45 to 60 / an hour or more. ES: de 20 a 30 minutos / de 30 a 45 / de 45 a 60 / una hora o más. Nothing frustrates a client faster than a session she never has time to finish.

**20. What do you actually have to train with?**  (required)

> ES: ¿Con qué cuentas de verdad para entrenar?

- answer type: pick any
- Options EN: a full gym / dumbbells / resistance bands / a bench / a barbell / machines / kettlebells / just my body. ES: un gym completo / mancuernas / bandas de resistencia / una banca / una barra / máquinas / pesas rusas / solo mi cuerpo. The app asks home or gym. This asks what is actually in the room, which is what decides whether a real glute program is even possible for her.

**21. Outside of workouts, how much do you move in a normal day?**

> ES: Fuera de los entrenamientos, ¿cuánto te mueves en un día normal?

- answer type: pick one
- Options EN: sitting most of the day / on my feet part of the day / on my feet all day / physical job. ES: sentada casi todo el día / de pie una parte del día / de pie todo el día / trabajo físico. Two women at the same weight with different jobs do not get the same calories.

**22. What have you tried before, and what made you stop?**  (required)

> ES: ¿Qué has intentado antes y qué hizo que lo dejaras?

- answer type: paragraph
- The most useful question on the whole form. It tells you exactly what to never put her through again, and it usually tells you what she is afraid of.

**23. Is anything coming up that will break your routine?**

> ES: ¿Viene algo que te va a romper la rutina?

- answer type: short answer
- Travel, a busy season at work, school starting, a move, a family visit. Plan around it now and it stops being the reason she disappears for three weeks.

## Food, honestly / La comida, con honestidad

*You cannot write her nutrition until you know what she really eats, who cooks it, and what she will never give up.*

**24. Any allergies, or foods you do not eat?**  (required)  · *already in the app*

> ES: ¿Tienes alergias o alimentos que no comes?

- answer type: pick any
- Options EN: dairy / gluten / eggs / nuts / shellfish / soy / pork / vegetarian / vegan / none of these, plus a box for anything else. ES: lácteos / gluten / huevo / nueces / mariscos / soya / puerco / vegetariana / vegana / ninguna de estas, y un espacio para lo demás. Already asked in the app. Keep it here anyway if you ever send this form on its own, because an allergy is a safety issue, not a preference.

**25. In a normal week, how much of your food do you cook yourself?**  (required)

> ES: En una semana normal, ¿cuánta de tu comida cocinas tú?

- answer type: pick one
- Options EN: almost all of it / about half / mostly takeout or restaurants / someone else cooks for me. ES: casi toda / como la mitad / casi todo es comida para llevar o restaurante / alguien más cocina para mí. This decides whether you send her recipes or restaurant swaps. 'Someone else cooks' usually means family food, and the plan has to work around what is already on the table.

**26. Walk me through what you eat on a normal day, from the morning to bedtime.**  (required)

> ES: Cuéntame qué comes en un día normal, desde que te levantas hasta que te duermes.

- answer type: paragraph
- Do not send anyone a plan before you read this. It is the fastest way to see meal timing, where her protein is missing, and where the calories are actually hiding.

**27. What foods are you not willing to give up?**  (required)

> ES: ¿Qué comidas no estás dispuesta a dejar?

- answer type: short answer
- Build these in on purpose from day one. A plan with no room for her mom's cooking is a plan she quits, and telling her she can keep them is the moment she believes you.

**28. How often do you drink alcohol?**

> ES: ¿Con qué frecuencia tomas alcohol?

- answer type: pick one
- Options EN: never / a few times a month / once or twice a week / most days / I would rather not say. ES: nunca / algunas veces al mes / una o dos veces por semana / casi todos los días / prefiero no decir. No judgment in how you ask it. It is calories she is not counting and sleep she is not getting.

**29. Have you counted macros or tracked your food before?**

> ES: ¿Has contado macros o registrado tu comida antes?

- answer type: pick one
- Options EN: never / I tried and it did not stick / yes, I am comfortable with it. ES: nunca / lo intenté y no me duró / sí, me siento cómoda con eso. Sets how much you teach in week one instead of assuming.

**30. How would you describe your relationship with food right now?**  · *already in the app*

> ES: ¿Cómo describirías tu relación con la comida ahora mismo?

- answer type: pick one
- Options EN: I am good with food / I have struggled with food or with my body / I would rather keep tracking light / I would rather not say. ES: estoy bien con la comida / he batallado con la comida o con mi cuerpo / prefiero llevar el registro ligero / prefiero no decir. Already asked in the app, and the app already softens her screens when she answers this way. Anything other than the first answer means no calorie talk in the first weeks, and a real eating disorder goes to a licensed professional, warmly and without making her feel rejected.

## Life outside the gym / La vida fuera del gym

*Sleep, stress and her actual schedule decide her results more than her program does, and they tell you where the workout can even fit.*

**31. How many hours do you usually sleep?**  (required)  · *already in the app*

> ES: ¿Cuántas horas duermes normalmente?

- answer type: pick one
- Options EN: under 5 / 5 to 6 / 7 to 8 / more than 8. ES: menos de 5 / de 5 a 6 / de 7 a 8 / más de 8. Already asked in the app.

**32. How is your stress most weeks?**  (required)  · *already in the app*

> ES: ¿Cómo está tu estrés la mayoría de las semanas?

- answer type: pick one
- Options EN: low / medium / high. ES: bajo / medio / alto. Already asked in the app. High stress plus a big deficit is how you break someone, so this changes how aggressive you get.

**33. What does a normal weekday look like for you? Work hours, kids, shifts.**

> ES: ¿Cómo es un día normal entre semana para ti? Horario de trabajo, hijos, turnos.

- answer type: short answer
- You are looking for the hole in her day where a workout actually fits, and for night shifts, which change everything about meal timing.

**34. What is most likely to get in the way over the next 12 weeks?**  (required)

> ES: ¿Qué es lo que más te puede estorbar en las próximas 12 semanas?

- answer type: paragraph
- Ask it now and it stops being a surprise later. Whatever she names here, build the backup plan for it in week one and tell her you did.

## How we work together / Cómo trabajamos juntas

*This is the retention section. It costs her four taps and it tells you how to keep her.*

**35. What day works best for your weekly check-in?**

> ES: ¿Qué día te queda mejor para tu revisión semanal?

- answer type: pick one
- Options EN: Monday / Tuesday / Wednesday / Thursday / Friday / weekend / no preference. ES: lunes / martes / miércoles / jueves / viernes / fin de semana / me da igual. Pick her day, not yours, and keep it the same every week.

**36. When you fall off, how do you want me to talk to you?**  (required)

> ES: Cuando te caigas, ¿cómo quieres que te hable?

- answer type: pick one
- Options EN: gently, remind me why I started / straight up, tell me what to fix / check on me more that week / give me space, I will come back. ES: con cariño, recuérdame por qué empecé / directo, dime qué corregir / búscame más esa semana / dame espacio, yo regreso. Nobody asks this and it is the difference between a woman who comes back and one who ghosts. Write her answer at the top of her file.

**37. Are you comfortable sending progress photos?**  (required)

> ES: ¿Te sientes cómoda mandando fotos de progreso?

- answer type: pick one
- Options EN: yes / yes, but only you see them / not yet, ask me again later / no. ES: sí / sí, pero solo tú las ves / todavía no, pregúntame más adelante / no. Photos are how you show progress when the scale is stuck, especially for anyone on a weight loss shot. Respect a no and use measurements instead.

**38. Anything else I should know about you?**

> ES: ¿Algo más que deba saber de ti?

- answer type: paragraph
- Leave it last and leave it open. This is where women put the real thing they did not have a box for.

## Before we start / Antes de empezar

*Four boxes that put in writing what she agreed to. This is the part that protects you, and it takes her ten seconds.*

**39. Everything I put here is true and complete, and I will tell Steph if anything changes.**  (required)

> ES: Todo lo que puse aquí es verdadero y completo, y le avisaré a Steph si algo cambia.

- answer type: yes / no
- Standard, and it matters. It puts the responsibility for accuracy where it belongs and gives you something dated on file.

**40. I understand this is coaching, not medical care, physical therapy or a clinical diet, and if I checked anything on the safety questions I will get my doctor's OK before I start.**  (required)

> ES: Entiendo que esto es coaching, no atención médica, ni terapia física, ni una dieta clínica, y si marqué algo en las preguntas de seguridad voy a pedir el visto bueno de mi doctor antes de empezar.

- answer type: yes / no
- This is the scope of practice line. Coaches train movement, food habits and consistency. Diagnosing injuries, prescribing rehab, treating eating disorders and writing clinical diets belong to licensed professionals, and several US states regulate individual nutrition counseling. Refer out and write down that you did.

**41. If I feel chest pain, dizziness or anything that does not feel right, I will stop and contact my doctor.**  (required)

> ES: Si siento dolor en el pecho, mareos o algo que no se siente bien, voy a parar y contactar a mi doctor.

- answer type: yes / no
- Say it once at intake so she never wonders whether she should push through. She should not.

**42. Your photos: what are you okay with?**  (required)

> ES: Tus fotos: ¿con qué estás de acuerdo?

- answer type: pick one
- Options EN: only my coach sees them / Steph can ask me before sharing any of them / Steph can share them. ES: solo mi coach las ve / Steph me puede preguntar antes de compartir alguna / Steph las puede compartir. Do not post a single transformation photo without this on file, and get a fresh yes for each specific post even from the women who said yes here.
