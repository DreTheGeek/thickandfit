## Part 2. Five things to decide before this goes up

These are real decisions, not paperwork. We are flagging them rather than quietly picking for you.

### Risk 1. Referral entries are the one thing that can turn this into a legal problem

Your page promises 3 extra entries for every friend who joins with someone's link. That is the
engine of the whole waitlist, and it is also the single riskiest thing in it. A giveaway stays legal
by being free. Several states read "recruiting your friends" as a form of payment, and once a
drawing has a prize, chance, and payment in any form, it stops being a giveaway and becomes a
lottery, which a private business cannot run.

Two ways to stay clean:

- **Option A, keep the mechanic and add a free path.** Anyone who does not want to refer friends can
  mail in for the same entries, free. That is what section 6 of the rules below does. It is the
  standard fix and it changes nothing about your page.
- **Option B, change what referrals earn.** Instead of drawing entries, referrals earn guaranteed
  rewards at milestones. Bring 3 friends and you get X, bring 10 and you get Y. Guaranteed rewards
  sit completely outside this area of law. This is the cleaner answer, and it costs a page change
  and a small build.

**We drafted Option A** because it matches what you already promised the women who are on the list
right now. If you would rather do Option B, say so and we will rewrite both the page and these
rules.

### Risk 2. Keep the total prize value under $5,000

New York and Florida both require you to register the giveaway with the state and post a bond if a
prize is worth more than $5,000. New York wants that 30 days before entries open, and entries are
already open, so that door is closed for this launch. Florida wants 7 days.

Under $5,000 total, neither applies and there is nothing to file. That is the recommendation.

### Risk 3. Instagram is currently required to enter, and it is required by the form, not just the copy

The waitlist form will not submit without an Instagram handle. The page explains it as "so we can @
you if you win." That is a legal thing you are allowed to require, and the rules below say so. It is
also the reason every woman who wants your app and does not use Instagram is turned away at the
door.

If you make the handle optional (there is a separate recommendation about this on the site change
list), sections 3 and 5 below change, and we will update them.

### Risk 4. The planned "founding members only" final drawing

The build has a third drawing that only includes people who paid to become founding members. A
drawing you can only enter by paying is exactly the arrangement the law here exists to prevent. The
rules below keep that drawing but attach a free entry route to it, so nobody has to buy anything to
be in it. If you would rather drop that drawing entirely, that is simpler and safer.

### Risk 5. This needs a Spanish version

Roughly half your list reads Spanish. If the rules are only in English, half the entrants cannot
read the terms they are agreeing to. When you approve this, we will have it translated properly, not
machine translated, and publish both.

#### 8. How the winner is picked

There are `[NUMBER]` drawings:

| Drawing | Date | Who is in it |
|---|---|---|
| Early bird | `[DRAW DATE]` | Everyone eligible who has entered and confirmed their email by that date |
| Birthday drawing | `[DRAW DATE]`, Sept 27 | Everyone eligible who has entered and confirmed their email by that date |
| Founding members | `[DRAW DATE]` | Founding members, plus anyone who entered free by mail per section 6 |

Winners are drawn at random, weighted by the number of entries each person has. More entries means
more chances, and it never means a guaranteed win.

**How the drawing actually works, since referrals are involved and you deserve to know it was
straight.** Before each drawing, we freeze the list of everyone eligible and how many entries each
person holds, and we take a digital fingerprint of that frozen list, so any later change to it would
be obvious. Then we generate a random number, called a seed, that nobody can predict beforehand and
that cannot be changed afterwards. The seed picks the winner from the frozen list. Because we keep
both the frozen list and the seed, anyone can rerun the exact same math and arrive at the exact same
winner. If a result is ever questioned, that is the answer, not "trust us."
