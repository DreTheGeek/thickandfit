# Your website, page by page

You did not have to write this list. We went through every page on the site, in English and in
Spanish, and wrote down what we would change and why.

**How to use it.** Everything in Section 1 is one question with a yes or no answer. The new wording is
already written for you. If you want a different word anywhere, just say the word and we will use it.
Section 2 is things we are simply fixing. You do not have to read it, it is there so you can see they
are handled.

**22 decisions.** Most of them will take you about ten seconds each.

# SECTION 1: YOUR CALL

## Home page

### 1. The buttons say "Start today" but the only thing anyone can do today is join a list. Change them?

**Now:** "Start today" (in 5 places) and "Cancel anytime, no contract."

**Change to:** Button: "Save my spot". Line under it: "Doors open Sept 27. Your founding price locks
the minute you join." Closing block: "This is the last time you start over. Get on the list. Founding
price locked for life, and I let you in first."

Right now the first click breaks the promise the page just made. And "cancel anytime" on a page where
there is nothing to cancel is exactly the billing trick you built this to be the opposite of.

### 2. Your name does not appear anywhere a visitor can see on the home page. Add it?

**Now:** Top bar: "Coaching in English and Spanish". Headline: "Every day, already decided."

**Change to:** Top bar: "Coach Stephanie Pantoja. In English and in Spanish." And a line under the
headline: "Coach Stephanie Pantoja. The same method my clients pay for, now in your pocket."

562,000 people follow you, not a brand name. Right now a woman coming straight off your Instagram
cannot confirm in five seconds that this is yours.

### 3. Confirm the founding window dates so we can put them on every page.

**Now:** The home page says "Founding pricing for life" with no second price and no deadline. The
pricing page says 5 days. Nowhere says which 5 days.

**Change to:** Everywhere: "$19.97 a month, locked for life. It goes to $24.97 the moment the five day
founding window closes. Get on the list and you get in first." Plus the actual dates: "The founding
window is 5 days: September 27 through October 1."

We think it is Sept 27 to Oct 1. Confirm it or correct it. A deadline with no date reads as invented,
and that is the thing that stops the card coming out.

### 4. Add your origin story to the home page, not just the About page?

**Now:** Nothing like this on the home page.

**Change to:** A short block after the four benefit tiles: "I built this because nothing I could buy
was built for us. I coached these women on someone else's platform for years. Every week the same
thing broke: the food. So I built my own. This is my method, my programming and my voice, on a
platform I own." Signed "Steph."

This is your single best answer to "another influencer cashing in," and right now it only lives on a
page most people never click.

### 5. Make the English and Spanish promise a real selling point instead of a ribbon at the top?

**Now:** "Coaching in English and Spanish" in the top bar, then nothing until the very bottom.

**Change to:** Add it as a benefit tile: "Write to me in the language you think in. Your plan, your
food, your coach, all of it in Spanish or in English. Not a translated menu over an English app. The
whole thing."

This is the one thing MyFitnessPal, Cal AI, Noom and Fitia all fail at. It is currently in the
smallest type on the page.

### 6. The home page FAQ prices a $200 tier nobody can buy yet. Drop it?

**Now:** "Foundation is $19.97 a month. Coached membership, where Steph and her team work with you
directly, starts at $200 a month."

founding window closes. If you want me working with you one on one, say so when you join the list and
I will reach out."

Naming a price with no way to buy it either scares people off the $19.97 or looks like a fake price
put there to make the cheap one look cheap.

## The comparison pages (vs MyFitnessPal, vs Cal AI, vs Fitia)

These are the pages built to catch women searching Google for your competitors. They are for people
who have never heard of you.

### 15. These pages sell "filmed demos." There are 2 videos across 899 exercises and neither is yours. Film them, or change the words?

**Now:** "Filmed demos, built around your equipment" and "workouts with Stephanie's filmed demos built
around the equipment you have."

**Change to, if the footage will not be ready by Sept 27:** "Her programming, not a generic plan" and
"training Stephanie programmed herself, her method guiding you every day, a community that notices
when you disappear, and a timeline that shows you changing, week by week. You are not doing this alone
anymore."

We put "her filmed demos, built around your equipment" back the day the videos land. This is the thing
people are paying for, so promising it before it exists is refunds, not a copy nit.

### 16. Two pages say "3 days free, no card to start." Neither half is true today. Which way do you want it?

**Now:** "Start 3 days free" and "No card to start. Cancel any time, one tap."

Two separate things here, and we should be straight with you about both.

The **3 day trial** you asked for in July is built. It is switched off with a setting, not missing.
Turning it on is a five minute job on our side, any day you say.

**"No card to start" is the harder one.** Stripe asks for the card up front even during a free trial.
We can turn that off, but then a trial can end with nobody's card on file, and everyone who forgot
just disappears instead of becoming a member. That is a real decision, not a technicality.

**Option A, our recommendation:** we switch the trial on, and the page says "3 days free. We save your
card, we show you the price first, and we tell you before we charge you." Honest, and it still beats
Cal AI, whose whole trick is hiding that second part.

**Option B:** no trial at all. Button says "Start today," fine print says "$19.97 a month, founding
price. Cancel in one tap from your account. No phone call."

We cannot leave it as it is. We are attacking Cal AI for a trial that tricks people, on the same page
where we describe a trial we do not currently have.

### 17. The Fitia page promises "her team checks in every week." Can your team hold that?

**Now:** "Her team checks in every week."

**Change to, if you would rather not commit to weekly:** "Her team is in your messages, not a help
desk." And in the FAQ: "her team in your messages when you need them."

There are 2 coaches for 265 members coming over. A stated frequency is something a member can hold you
to, and a missed week on a page that sold the week is the fastest route to a refund request. Fitia has
no humans at all, so the softer line still wins the comparison.

### 18. These pages sell "Stephanie" to people who have never heard of you. Add a line saying who you are?

**Now:** "Fitia counts. Stephanie coaches." Your name appears with no explanation on all three pages.

**Change to:** A band under the headline: "Stephanie Pantoja is a certified nutrition coach who has

information. They needed someone who stayed." Then one member line with her photo.

To a woman who found this page on Google, "Stephanie coaches" currently reads as "a stranger coaches."

## FAQ and legal

### 19. Nothing public says what happens to her money if it does not work out. Approve this answer?

**Now:** The FAQ only says "No free trial, but no contract either." The actual refund policy only
appears after she pays.

**Change to:** A new FAQ item: "**What if it turns out not to be for me?** You cancel in one tap from
your Account, any time, and you keep everything through the end of the month you already paid for. We
don't refund a month that's already been used, because you had the whole app that whole time. If you
get charged by mistake, tell us on the Support page and we'll fix it that day. No arguing, no forms to
chase."

No trial plus no stated refund policy puts all of the risk on her at the exact second she decides. And
a billing term she only discovers after paying is how chargebacks get made.

### 20. Add three answers the FAQ is missing: when it opens, whether Latin America can pay, and that it renews?

**Now:** No launch date on the FAQ at all. Prices shown as "$19.97" with no currency. The cancel
answer says "no hidden auto-renew traps," which a lot of people will read as "it does not renew."

**Change to:** Three additions.

"**When does it open, and what happens if I join the list now?** Doors open September 27, Stephanie's
birthday. Joining the list costs nothing today. You get your spot in line and a message the moment it
opens. Founding pricing is the first five days only: join in that window and you keep $19.97 a month
for as long as you stay. After those five days it's $24.97."

"**Can I join from Mexico, Colombia, or anywhere else in Latin America?** Yes. Prices are in US
dollars and any card that works internationally works here, debit included. Your bank does the
conversion. If your card gets declined, message us on the Support page and we'll work it out with
you."

And the cancel answer becomes: "Yes. Your membership renews every month until you cancel, and you
cancel in one tap from your Account. No phone call, no retention maze, nobody talking you out of it.
You keep access through the end of the month you already paid for."

Half your audience is in Latin America, where a bare "$" reads as pesos. And saying the renewal out
loud is what kills the "I didn't know it would charge me again" dispute before it happens.

### 21. Answer "is Stephanie the one answering me?" before someone works it out from the privacy page.

**Now:** No answer anywhere. The privacy page discloses the technology, so that is where people
currently find out.

**Change to:** "**Is Stephanie the one answering me in the app?** Your coach in the app is her method,
her programming and her words, working around the clock so you're not waiting three days for an
answer. It's built on how she actually coaches, and she and her team are behind it. Anything that
needs a person reaches one, and you can always message the team from Support."

Two things to confirm: that this is how you want it said, and that "anything that needs a person
reaches one" matches how your team will really work.

### 22. Approve interim Terms so the page is not blank while people are agreeing to it.

**Now:** "We are finalizing the full text of our Terms of Service ahead of public launch."

**Change to:** "The full legal text is with our attorney and lands before the doors open. What's below
already binds us both, and it will not change when the long version publishes. Your membership renews
every month at the price you joined at, until you cancel. You cancel in one tap under Account and keep
access through the end of the month you paid for. Months you've already used aren't refunded. If we
ever charge you in error, we fix it. This is fitness and nutrition coaching, not medical care. Read
the health notice before you train. Treat the women here the way you'd want to be treated. Harassment,
spam and selling get you removed. Questions before the full version publishes? Ask us on the Support
page."

Sign up already makes people agree to this document, and the document currently says it does not exist
yet. It is also the page that would legally back the "no tricks" promise in your FAQ.
