# Living Spec: Nutrition (REQ-NUTR-*)

## REQ-NUTR-001: Macro calculator
THE SYSTEM SHALL compute TDEE and goal-based macro targets, coach-editable per client.
Added by: PRD-13 | Status: planned

## REQ-NUTR-002: Food database
THE SYSTEM SHALL resolve foods against USDA + Open Food Facts + a curated LATAM ingredient layer,
with bilingual search.
Added by: PRD-14 | Status: planned

## REQ-NUTR-003: Food logging + barcode
WHEN a client logs food THE SYSTEM SHALL support search, barcode scan, and manual entry, and roll
up daily macros vs targets. Barcode is not paywalled.
Added by: PRD-15 | Status: planned

## REQ-NUTR-004: Cooked/uncooked conversion
WHEN a client logs a food in cooked or raw state THE SYSTEM SHALL convert to the coach's
programmed basis using a deterministic ratio table.
Added by: PRD-16 | Status: planned

## REQ-NUTR-005: AI photo-to-macro
WHEN a client photographs a meal THE SYSTEM SHALL return foods, estimated portions, macros, and a
confidence score. Metered 5/day on low tier, unlimited on mid-ticket.
Added by: PRD-17 | Status: planned

## REQ-NUTR-006: Meal plans + recipes
THE SYSTEM SHALL let a coach build meal plans with macro targets and assign recipes, with a
bilingual recipe library populated by the in-house recipe engine.
Added by: PRD-18, PRD-19, PRD-20 | Status: planned
