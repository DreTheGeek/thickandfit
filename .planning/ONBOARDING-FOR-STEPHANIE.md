# Your signup questions

Three minutes. Nothing here needs a technical answer.

I went through the signup flows of nine apps you compete with: Noom, Cal AI, Fitia in Spanish,
MacroFactor, Ladder, Caliber, and the women's apps like Sweat and Flo. 322 questions between them.
Then I looked at what a woman answers when she joins you.

**Here is the opportunity.** Every one of those nine asks a woman how many days a week she can train
and what equipment she has, before writing her anything. That is the difference between a program
built for her and a program handed to her. It is nine extra taps and it is the single biggest upgrade
available to your signup.

Below are nine questions I want to add, written out and ready. Cross out any that are not you.

---

## The nine

**I recommend yes on all nine.** Each one is written in your voice, in both languages, with the little
line underneath that makes it feel like you asking rather than a form.

### 1. Days per week

The one that changes the program most.

> **EN:** "Honestly, how many days a week can you train?" 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 or more
> *"Give me the honest number, not the ambitious one. I would rather build three days you actually hit."*
>
> **ES:** "Con honestidad, ¿cuántos días a la semana puedes entrenar?" 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 o más
> *"Dime el número honesto, no el ambicioso. Prefiero armarte tres días que sí cumplas."*

### 2. How long a session can be

A workout she cannot finish is the fastest way to lose her.

> **EN:** "And how long can a workout actually be?" 20 to 30 min / 30 to 45 / 45 to 60 / An hour or more
>
> **ES:** "¿Y cuánto tiempo puede durar un entrenamiento, de verdad?" de 20 a 30 min / de 30 a 45 / de 45 a 60 / una hora o más

### 3. Equipment

"At home" is either a garage rack or a yoga mat, and a glute program is either possible or it is not.

> **EN:** "What do you actually have to train with?" A full gym / Dumbbells / Resistance bands / A bench / A barbell / Machines / Kettlebells / Just my body
> *"Pick everything you can get to. If it is just you and the floor, that is enough and I will build for it."*
>
> **ES:** "¿Con qué cuentas de verdad para entrenar?" Un gym completo / Mancuernas / Bandas de resistencia / Una banca / Una barra / Máquinas / Pesas rusas / Solo mi cuerpo
> *"Marca todo a lo que puedas llegar. Si son solo tú y el piso, con eso basta y así te lo armo."*

### 4. What she has already tried

This is the one that lets you talk to her in week six like you remember her.

> **EN:** "What have you tried before, and what made you stop?"
> *"Say it however it comes out. This is the part that tells me what to never put you through again."*
>
> **ES:** "¿Qué has intentado antes y qué hizo que lo dejaras?"
> *"Dilo como te salga. Esta es la parte que me dice qué no volver a hacerte pasar."*

### 5. After birth

**None of the nine apps asks this.** Your audience is women with children, and it decides whether her
first week has crunches and planks in it or not. Only appears if she says postpartum.

> **EN:** "Since you gave birth, have you noticed any of these?"
> *"If you have, you are not alone. These are common, and they change what we do first, not whether we do it."*
> A bulge or a ridge down the middle of my belly when I sit up / Leaking when I jump, cough or sneeze / Heaviness or pressure down low / Back or hip pain / None of these
> Then: "Has your doctor cleared you to exercise?" Yes / Not yet
>
> **ES:** "Desde que diste a luz, ¿has notado alguna de estas?"
> *"Si sí, no eres la única. Son comunes, y cambian qué hacemos primero, no si lo hacemos."*
> Un bulto o una línea que salta en medio del abdomen cuando me siento / Se me sale un poco de orina al saltar, toser o estornudar / Sensación de peso o presión abajo / Dolor de espalda o cadera / Ninguna de estas
> Luego: "¿Tu doctor ya te dio permiso para hacer ejercicio?" Sí / Todavía no

### 6. Weight-loss shots

More of your women are on these than will mention it. It changes her calories, and it means a flat
week gets read as a dose change instead of as her failing.

> **EN:** "Are you using a weight-loss shot right now?" No / Yes / Prefer not to say
> Then: "Which one, what dose, and when did you start?"
>
> **ES:** "¿Estás usando alguna inyección para bajar de peso?" No / Sí / Prefiero no decir
> Luego: "¿Cuál es, qué dosis y cuándo empezaste?"

### 7. How she wants you to talk to her when she falls off

Four taps, and it sets the tone of every message she gets from then on.

> **EN:** "When you fall off, how do you want me to talk to you?" Gently, remind me why I started / Straight up, tell me what to fix / Check on me more that week / Give me space, I will come back
>
> **ES:** "Cuando te caigas, ¿cómo quieres que te hable?" Con cariño, recuérdame por qué empecé / Directo, dime qué corregir / Búscame más esa semana / Dame espacio, yo regreso

### 8. The food she is keeping

The cheapest trust in the whole signup.

> **EN:** "What are you not willing to give up?"
> *"Tell me now and I build around it. A plan with no room for your mom's cooking is a plan you quit."*
>
> **ES:** "¿Qué comidas no estás dispuesta a dejar?"
> *"Dímelo desde ahora y lo armo alrededor. Un plan sin espacio para la comida de tu mamá es un plan que dejas."*

### 9. Photo permission

This is what lets you post her transformation without having to go back and ask.

> **EN:** "Your progress photos: what are you okay with?" Only my coach sees them / Ask me before sharing any / You can share them
>
> **ES:** "Tus fotos de progreso: ¿con qué estás de acuerdo?" Solo mi coach las ve / Pregúntame antes de compartir alguna / Las puedes compartir

## Three that are yours to call

**Her first number.** A shorter, less active member is honestly looking at around half a pound a week.
That is a real and safe pace, and the lever that actually speeds it up is moving more, not eating
less. I would rather say that to her than show her a distant date and let her draw her own conclusion.

> **EN:** "At your activity level this is the pace I can promise safely. Add a daily walk and this moves faster than any smaller portion will."
> **ES:** "Con tu nivel de actividad, este es el ritmo que te puedo prometer con seguridad. Agrega una caminata diaria y esto avanza más rápido que cualquier porción más pequeña."

**The "tell me anything" box.** It promises, in your voice, that you read all of it. At 265 members
that is about a day of reading, and it is a promise a member will hold you to.

- **Keep it** and you or Dani read them. We will put them all in one place so it is an hour, not a hunt.
- **Or reword it** to something just as warm that always stays true: *"Nothing here gets lost. It goes straight onto your file."* / *"Nada de esto se pierde. Va directo a tu expediente."*

Recommendation: reword it. You will want that day back in September.

**One phrase.** One of the options a woman can tap says *"I've struggled with disordered eating."*
That asks her to put a clinical label on herself in a list of buttons. Your Spanish is already
gentler. Recommendation for the English: *"Food and I have had a hard history."*

## What is not changing, and why

**She sees her plan before she sees a price.** Every tracker paywalls first. You are not a tracker.

**The health questions stay before the payment**, even though they cost us a few signups. You do real
programming and real one-on-one, so we have to know about her knees and her pregnancy before we write
her anything. That trade is worth it.

**Not yet, but on the list:** her cycle and perimenopause, what country she is in, and letting her
read the app in English while she searches her food in Spanish.
