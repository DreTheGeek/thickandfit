# Physique Read, Safety Review for Stephanie

**Status:** Built and wired, NOT live to members yet. It stays off until you sign off on the rules below.
**What you are approving:** the exact bounds the AI must follow when it reads a member's progress photo.
**Time to review:** about 5 minutes. You do not need to read any code. Just react to the rules and the example.

---

## What this feature does

A member opens her Progress Photos, taps "Coach's read" on one of her photos, and gets back, in your
voice and her language (English or Spanish):

1. A body-fat **range** estimate (never a single exact number).
2. What is genuinely working in her physique.
3. One to three gentle areas to focus on (never called flaws).
4. A staged, realistic plan toward her goal.
5. A short, warm write-up that sounds like you.

It uses her reported weight and goal to ground the read, not just the photo. Progress photos are private:
only the member and her coach can see them, and now this read.

---

## The hard safety rules the AI must follow (please approve or change each)

1. **Never shame or judge.** No "flaws," no "fixing your body." Always leads with what is working.
2. **Body fat is always a RANGE** (for example "around 24 to 28%"), always called a coach's-eye estimate,
   and explicitly **not a medical or DEXA measurement**. Never a single precise number.
3. **Body-positive and goal-relative.** Everything is anchored to HER goal and her health, not an
   idealized standard. Strong and healthy over skinny.
4. **Eating-disorder aware.** Never encourages restriction, "earning" food, purging, or obsession. If the
   photo or context suggests possible disordered eating, very low body fat, or distress, it quietly
   **flags the read for you to follow up** and gently suggests she check in with you (and a professional if
   relevant). It does NOT diagnose.
5. **No medical claims.** Anything clinical (PCOS, hormones, thyroid, injury, pregnancy) is deferred to a
   healthcare professional.
6. **Realistic and kind.** Stages the plan into achievable milestones, celebrates the body she has today,
   and emphasizes physique and health over the scale number.

## Your coaching voice (please confirm this sounds like you)

Meet her where she is. Reframe goals positively. Build on her strengths and what her body already does
well. If she has been leaner or stronger before, use that (muscle memory, she already knows how). Give a
realistic staged path and a simple sustainable approach (movement, protein, consistency). End on real
belief in her.

> You can refine this voice and philosophy yourself later through the AI Knowledge builder, with no code
> change. Your notes there feed directly into this read.

and the flag shows up on your side.
