# The 2-week Whole Foods Anti-Inflammatory Reset

Transcribed verbatim from the PDF Daniella sent Shayna on 5 Aug 2026. This is Stephanie's IP and the
source of truth for the protocol template. **Do not paraphrase her rules when building from this.**

## What it is, structurally

Not a meal plan. A **protocol**: fixed rules plus one fixed day, repeated for 14 days, identical for
every client. The header says **"EVERYONE must follow"**, so it is a template, not a personalisation.

That distinction is the whole reason our meal-plan builder cannot hold it. The builder assumes
per-client macro targets and slot-level recipe variation. This assumes the opposite: one day, no
variation, and **"Consistency > variety"** stated as the point rather than a limitation.

It is step one of a two-step onboarding. Daniella's covering message: *"After you complete the full 2
weeks of the anti-inflammatory plan, Coach Steph will create and deliver your personalized meal plan
directly here in the app."*

## Purpose, in her words

> This reset is designed to support fat loss, reduce inflammation, curb sugar cravings and build
> discipline through whole foods and consistency.

## The 12 non-negotiable rules

1. Weigh oats, eggwhites and green beans **UNCOOKED**.
2. Weigh everything else **COOKED**.
3. **Proteins** are listed in **ounces cooked**.
4. **Carbs** and **fruit** are listed in **grams cooked**.
5. **NO cooking oils.** Use spray only.
6. Suggested sprays: coconut oil spray, olive oil spray, or avocado oil spray.
7. Only packaged food allowed: Oikos Triple Zero yogurt. **NO PROTEIN SHAKES OR BARS OR CHIPS**
   during this phase.
8. Season food as you please (salt, pepper, garlic, paprika, lemon, herbs, etc).
9. Drink **80 to 128 oz of water daily**.
10. If you feel hungry, **you may add extra NON-STARCH vegetables** (spinach, greens, zucchini,
    peppers, broccoli).
11. **Do NOT add extra starches** (no potatoes, rice, oats, or fruit beyond what is listed).
12. **Eat all 5 meals daily.** Consistency > variety.

## What the design tells us about her method

Worth encoding, because these are the generalisable rules, not the specific foods:

- **Elimination, not deprivation.** 13 distinct ingredients. Hunger has a sanctioned answer (rule 10,
  unlimited non-starch veg) so the plan never requires white-knuckling.
- **Weighing convention is the most error-prone thing in any plan**, so it is rules 1 to 4, before
  anything else, and it is stated per food group rather than left to the client.
- **The only branded items are Oikos Triple Zero and Godshall's.** Both are specific because both are
  the low-sugar / low-additive option in a category where the default is not.
- **No protein powder, bars or chips** is the anti-inflammatory thesis in one rule: it removes the
  processed foods a fitness client is most likely to believe are compliant.
- **Gut support first thing, fasted**, before any food.
- **Fixed day, 14 times.** The discipline is the deliverable.
