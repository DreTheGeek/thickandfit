# Thick & Fit: Identity Positioning

Client direction, 2026-07-22. This supersedes feature-led marketing copy everywhere it conflicts.
Read with `STEPHANIE-VOICE-BIBLE.md`: the Voice Bible governs HOW she sounds, this governs WHAT we
sell.

---

## The thesis

Don't sell features. Sell identity. Sell transformation. Sell the future version of the customer.

When someone lands on Thick & Fit they must not think "this is another workout tracker." They must
think **"this is who I become."**

Remove the fitness-app feeling entirely. The product is not a tracker, it is the system that turns
someone into the person they have been trying to become.

## What she is actually buying

Not protein logs. Nobody wakes up wanting to log protein. She wants:

confidence, attention, energy, discipline, and to like what she sees in the mirror.

The feelings to engineer:
- "I am finally taking control."
- "I am becoming someone I am proud of."
- "I am building discipline."
- "I am not chasing motivation anymore."
- "This is my new identity."

## The translation table (feature to identity)

| Feature language (banned) | Identity language (use) |
|---|---|
| Track your calories | Become the person people ask for advice. |
| Workout tracking | Never wonder what to do when you walk into the gym again. |
| Meal logging | Eat with confidence instead of guilt. |
| Progress photos | Watch yourself become unrecognizable. |
| Drop 20 pounds | Walk into any room feeling like you belong there. |
| All-in-one fitness solution | The system that helps you become the version of yourself you have been trying to become. |

Weight loss is a punishment frame. Thick & Fit sells becoming attractive, capable, disciplined and
confident. Never "fix yourself" energy.

## Why people actually fail

Competitors assume people fail from lack of information, so they sell tracking. People fail from
lack of **consistency**. The page has to hit that.

## Numbers and proof

**Do not publish counts that reveal scale.** No client totals, no follower totals as primary proof.
A serious company does not advertise how many customers it has; quantifying it invites the reader to
judge the size. Proof comes from named client voices, specificity and confidence of tone, never from
a scoreboard.

## Language bans

- No "AI" anywhere user-facing. People do not care. The experience simply feels smart.
  (Reinforces the existing brand rule: it is her method, automated where it helps.)
- No feature-brochure language. No boring SaaS wording. No "all-in-one fitness solution."
- No metrics that quantify company size.

## Visual register

Show the software like a luxury product, not screenshots dumped into cards:
cinematic dashboard shots, floating UI, premium transitions, large typography, lifestyle imagery.
**Porsche configurator, not MyFitnessPal.**

## Membership framing

Sell a Transformation Membership, not a monthly fitness app. Levels:
- **Foundation**: build consistency.
- **Evolution**: the full transformation system.
- **Elite**: maximum accountability.

It should feel like joining something.

## Product direction (beyond the landing page)

Logged-in surfaces should show identity, not tools. Tools require discipline; identity creates it.

**Navigation rename:**
| Now | Becomes | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | Today | the daily command center |
| Workouts | Train | everything physical |
| Nutrition | Fuel | meals, macros, recipes, scans |
| Progress | Evolution | the transformation timeline |
| Community | Community | the social layer, the retention weapon |

**Dashboard as command center.** It opens like a luxury car dashboard, not a spreadsheet. Not
"Calories: 1,842". Instead: a greeting, TODAY'S MISSION with a completion percentage, then TRAIN /
FUEL / MOVE / RECOVER, then YOUR EVOLUTION with week count, photos, measurements, strength. The user
should feel "I am winning" when they open it.

**Onboarding opens emotionally**, not with weight. First question: "Who are you becoming?" with
options (stronger, more confident, healthier, more disciplined, completely transformed). Collect the
numbers after the dream is sold.

**Transformation timeline** ("My Evolution") is the highest-retention feature: Week 1 "I started",
Week 4 "Clothes fit differently", Week 8 "People are noticing", Week 12 "I don't recognize myself".
People quit because they do not notice change.
